How this page is calculated

Daily gainers and decliners use reported daily percentage changes. Forecast coverage uses PreviewIndex 30-day estimates; FullIndex uses a 60-day horizon. Missing values remain unavailable rather than being filled with synthetic values.

PREFERENCES & TRANSPARENCY

Settings

Account controls, data sources, index coverage and application behaviour.

Account & synchronisation

Account

You are browsing as a guest. Sign in to sync your watchlist.

Watchlist items are tied to your account. Guest browsing remains available.

Forecast systems

Two index levels
PreviewIndex5 years of history, 30-trading-day forecast, and smaller model candidate set.
FullIndexUp to ten years of history with a 60-trading-day forecast and broader ensemble.

Directional validation measures one-day direction, not 30-day target accuracy.

Market data source

External quotes
Quote providerYahoo Finance chart endpoints with a secondary Yahoo host fallback.
Local snapshotD1 stores the latest successful quote so a provider error does not have to blank the dashboard.

Source and quote timestamps are shown when the API supplies them.

Refresh & caching

Automatic
Open-tab refreshMarket data refreshes every 90 seconds while the page is open.
Quote cacheFresh quotes are reused for five minutes; when refreshes are throttled, the saved D1 snapshot is served.

A feed refresh timestamp is not the same as an individual quote timestamp.

Privacy & data

Account details, sessions and saved watchlists are stored in the application database. Shared learning records contain aggregate model-performance information, not account-specific watchlist contents.

Session controlsSigned-in users can change their password or sign out from the account panel.

Validation & limitations

Model metrics use chronological holdout windows. A directional hit rate does not describe forecast error magnitude or guarantee future performance.

Missing valuesUnavailable prices, fundamentals or model diagnostics remain blank rather than being estimated.

Research disclaimer

Finanx is an educational research tool, not financial advice. Forecasts are uncertain and should not be relied upon as the sole basis for financial decisions. Market data may be delayed, incomplete or inaccurate. Independently verify information and consider risks.
