Data and forecasts

PreviewIndex uses approximately five years of daily history. FullIndex uses up to ten years and a larger ensemble. Both use chronological validation and deployment-wide model performance statistics. Accuracy varies by instrument, market regime and forecast horizon.

Privacy

Account details and watchlists are stored in the application database. Shared learning records store aggregate model performance, not account-specific watchlist contents.

Research disclaimer

For informational and educational purposes only. Not investment advice. Data can be delayed, incomplete or inaccurate. Forecasts are uncertain and should not be relied on as the sole basis for financial decisions.
